    How to play

    Guess the mystery footballer in 5 tries. Every player has played in a top-5 European league since 2010.

    Each guess shows how that player compares to the mystery player:

    Green: exact match
    Yellow: close
    Grey: no match
    • Nation: yellow means the same continent.
    • Pos: yellow means the same line (e.g. a defender, but a different defensive role).
    • Leagues: how many of the guessed player's leagues the mystery player also played in. Yellow means some overlap.
    • Clubs: how many of the guessed player's clubs the mystery player also played for.
    • Under each guess, every league and club is listed. The green ones are shared with the mystery player.
    • Born: yellow means within 2 years. ↑ means the mystery player was born later, ↓ earlier.

    Data: Transfermarkt, via transfermarkt-datasets.
